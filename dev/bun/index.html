once i get back into developing bun 🐰, further app-specific information will be available..

❓ some background information

  • bun 🐰 was my first every discord app all the back from 2019!
  • through many iterations of....well, awfully-written code to say the least, i decided to shelve it due to ambitions for making a more unique app: fox bot 🦊
    • what do i mean by "shelve"?
    • well, there were many discord apps which fundamentally did the same thing as bun 🐰's planned features
    • this would likely mean that the app probably wouldn't take off and thus not fulfilling my desires enough ehe
    • i do plan to return to bun 🐰! i will first need to review what would make the app more unique first
  • however, bun 🐰 was an important part of me actually learning to code in the first place! without the app's creation, i would not be as strong of a programmer as i was today~
