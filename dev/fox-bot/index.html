Overview

🦊 fox bot is an app specifically designed for all types of fox content!

🖼️ fox images!

  • see, share, and favourite a growing collection of 2,000+ unique fox images

📰 automatic fox images!

  • set up custom schedules for fox images in your server
  • these schedules can be hourly, daily, or every x hours~

💭 fox facts!

  • learn 100+ pieces of completely random fox trivia that you probably won't ever need to hear in your life to flex to your friends*
    *if you have any, that is..
  • browse the encyclopaedia all about foxes in a format that's pretty easy to understand
  • test your own knowledge about the facts you've learned in a quiz setting

🎮 virtual foxes!

  • collect virtual foxes through interactions you make to the app to create a mini skulk of foxes
  • show off how dedicated you are to the foxes with tracked statistics
  • contribute by uploading your own collections of fox images

🗃️ add fox bot anywhere!

  • add the app to servers for the ability to create fox image schedules
  • or, add the app to yourself to share fox images with others and use fox bot anywhere

How do I get started?

📋 view all commands through discord's command picker!

💬 type / in chat and select fox bot to get started~

Final Things!

💭 inspired by the many (now unfortunately disappearing) hourly animal image bots found on twitter, whose purpose were to send images and make people's day brighter!

💖 fox bot is made with user-experience and accessibility in mind~
